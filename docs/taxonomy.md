# Taxonomy

**Folders (5):** AI, AI for UXR, Legal AI, Public Sector, Research. A thread goes to the folder of its primary subject, regardless of who posted it.

**Thread types:** post, comment-thread, profile, article.

**Naming:** `Author - Topic - N`. Author is the author of the post (never a commenter); company posts use the company name; articles use the publication. Topics are short, in the language of the content, and never contain the platform name. No em dashes; use " - ".

**Tags** are free but kept consistent. Examples: `ai-agent-architecture`, `research-methodology-rigor`, `ai-vs-human-research`, `ai-trust-calibration`, `ai-governance-regulation`, `uxr-strategist-skills-framework`, `legal-tech-conferences`, `claude-code-practices`.
